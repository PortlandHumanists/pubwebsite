From FH. Cascade Life Alliance is one of 55 Organ Procurement Organizations (OPOs) in the United States, serving Oregon, Southwest Washington, and Southwest Idaho. This presentation will explain the organ, eye, and tissue donation process, the life-saving impact that one donor can make, common myths and misconceptions, and the many factors that influence people's decision-making process regarding donation registration. We aim to create a pressure-free, comfortable environment in which you can ask questions and share your concerns openly and without judgment.
