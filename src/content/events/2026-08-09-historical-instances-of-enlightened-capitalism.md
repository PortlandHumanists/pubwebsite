From FH, HGP member Al Christians will present short case studies of outstanding efforts to align business with human values. Don't miss these fabulous epic tales of good (and not so good) fighting it out in the executive suites. Al has a BS in Economics from Cal Tech and 30 years experience in actuarial work, including life and health insurance,  private pensions,  tax reporting and valuations for many insurance companies. He developed software for financial reporting by local governments, and calculation of payments on 40% of the autos sold in the US.
