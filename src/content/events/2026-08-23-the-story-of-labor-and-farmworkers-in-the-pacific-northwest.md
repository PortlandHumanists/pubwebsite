From FH. Friends of Seasonal and Service Workers (FSSW) will talk about the history of labor and farmworkers in the Pacific Northwest, how we got to the point we are at now and how people can get involved in changing these conditions. FSSW is a support effort for membership organizations of service workers, temporary workers, farm workers and others who are low income, uniting to end poverty conditions. They are 100% volunteer run and independent of government funding.
