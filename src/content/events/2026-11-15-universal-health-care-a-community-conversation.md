As the only state with a constitutional amendment guaranteeing healthcare as a fundamental right, Oregon is on track to be the first state to transition to a universal, single-payer system for every resident, expected on the ballot in 2028 after years of work. The Universal Health Plan Governance Board is delivering their recommendation to the OR legislature this December. Collin Stackhouse, of Health Care for All Oregon (HCAO) and a Universal Health Plan Governance Board committee, will tell us how the plan has been built, what universal healthcare actually is, and how to get further involved.'
